Privacy Policy

App: SnapSort: Screenshot Organizer (shown on your phone as SnapSort AI)
Package: com.mikao.screenshotorganizer on Google Play
Developer: Mikao · Contact: hclodhran1@gmail.com
Effective date: 27 September 2026

In short: SnapSort: Screenshot Organizer ("SnapSort AI", "the app"), made by Mikao ("we", "us"), reads the screenshots on your phone to sort them and make them searchable. All of that work happens on your device. Your screenshots, the text read from them and everything you add in the app are not uploaded to us, and we do not sell your data. The app uses the internet only to show ads (Google AdMob) and to check Google Play for app updates.

What the app uses on your phone, and why

Permission / dataWhy it's used
Photos and images (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, selected photos, or storage access on older Android versions)To find the screenshots on your device, show them in the app, read the text in them and sort them into categories such as receipts, bills, chats and OTPs. If you allow access to selected photos only, the app works with just those.
NotificationsTo tell you when new screenshots have been sorted, and for reminders and the optional daily or weekly summaries you can turn off in Settings.
Biometrics (fingerprint / face unlock)To unlock the private vault (a PIN can be used instead). The app never receives your fingerprint or face data; Android only tells it whether unlocking succeeded.
Internet and network stateTo load ads and to check Google Play for a newer version of the app. Your screenshots are never sent over the internet by the app.

Information processed on your device

To sort and search your screenshots, the app creates the following and keeps it in its private storage on your phone:

  • A list of your screenshots (file reference, date, size) and small thumbnails.
  • The text recognised in each screenshot, using on-device text recognition (Google ML Kit and Tesseract language models bundled with the app).
  • The category chosen for each screenshot, and details found in it such as receipt amounts, dates, QR code contents and one-time codes.
  • Quality scores used by Smart Cleanup to spot duplicates and blurry shots.
  • Anything you add yourself: tags, notes, favourites, reminders, collections and your settings.

This information is not uploaded to Mikao or to anyone else. We do not have servers that receive it.

Private vault

Screenshots you move to the vault are encrypted on your device with AES-256, using a key kept in the Android Keystore on your phone. Opening the vault requires your fingerprint or face unlock, or your vault PIN. Vault files are excluded from Android cloud backups and device-to-device transfers.

Sharing and export

The app shares a screenshot, text, a PDF export or a link to the app only when you choose to, using the Android share sheet. What happens to shared content then depends on the app you pick to receive it.

Android backup

If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may back up the app's settings and its on-device database (for example your tags, notes and categories) to your own Google account, so they can be restored on a new phone. These backups are managed by Google and your device, not by Mikao. Vault files are never included.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Everything the app stores stays on your phone until you delete it. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes it all.
  • The app deletes or moves your screenshot files only when you ask it to, and Android asks you to confirm first.
  • Because we do not collect your data, there is nothing held by Mikao to delete. If you have questions, email us at the address below.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

The app's data is kept in its private storage, which other apps cannot read, and vault items are encrypted as described above. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so please keep your phone protected with a screen lock.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes on Google Play.
